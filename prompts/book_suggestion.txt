## Your Task
Suggest exactly {num_suggestions} books this reader would love. Each suggestion must be carefully chosen.

## Analysis Framework

Before suggesting, analyze:

1. **RECENCY PATTERNS**: What have they read in the last few months? Recent reads reveal current mood and interests.

2. **GENRE DISTRIBUTION**: What genres dominate? Literary fiction, fantasy, sci-fi, thriller, romance, mystery, non-fiction? Look for patterns and diversity.

3. **AUTHOR LOYALTY**: Do they return to certain authors? This suggests they enjoy particular writing styles or ongoing series.

4. **SERIES VS STANDALONES**: Do they read book series or prefer standalone novels?

5. **COMPLEXITY & LENGTH**: Dense literary works or quick page-turners? Epic sagas or shorter novels?

6. **THEMES**: Recurring themes (family, identity, adventure, social issues, historical periods)?

7. **PACING**: Fast-paced thrillers or slow-burn character studies?

## Suggestion Strategy

- **60% Similar**: Books very aligned with their demonstrated preferences
- **30% Adjacent**: Slightly different but logically connected (e.g., same author's other work, adjacent genre)
- **10% Discovery**: One book that stretches their taste based on patterns you detect

## Critical Rules

- NEVER suggest books already in their library
- NEVER suggest books marked as REJECTED
- NEVER suggest books on their to-read list (they already know about those)
- Prioritize quality and fit over popularity
- Consider if an author they love has other works they haven't read

## Series Handling Rules (VERY IMPORTANT)

1. **Always suggest individual books, NEVER trilogies/series as a whole**
   - BAD: "The Lord of the Rings trilogy by Tolkien"
   - GOOD: "The Fellowship of the Ring by J.R.R. Tolkien"

2. **Only suggest Book 1 of any series**
   - Always suggest the FIRST book in a series, not book 2, 3, etc.
   - In the Summary, mention it's "Book 1 of [Series Name]" so the reader knows

3. **Series detection from reading history**
   - If user has read Book 2, 3, 4+ of a series, they have DEFINITELY read Book 1
   - NEVER suggest earlier books in a series if they've read later books
   - Example: If they've read "A Clash of Kings", do NOT suggest "A Game of Thrones"

4. **Continuing a series they're reading**
   - If they're reading Book 1-2 of a series, you MAY suggest the next unread book
   - But this counts as one of your suggestions and must follow the format
