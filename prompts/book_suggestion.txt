3. **Assess Complexity**: Do they read dense literary works or lighter reads? Long epics or shorter novels?

4. **Detect Themes**: What recurring themes appear (identity, family, adventure, social issues)?

5. **Consider Series vs Standalones**: Do they prefer series or standalone novels?

6. **Note Recency**: Recent reads indicate current interests. Give these more weight.

- Suggest books they have NOT already read (check against their library below)

- Include a mix: 2-3 similar to their favorites, 1-2 that stretch their tastes slightly
- Each suggestion must include a specific, personalized reason connecting to their history
- Be specific: mention which of their books inspired each recommendation
